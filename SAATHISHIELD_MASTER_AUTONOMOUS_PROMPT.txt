2. PRODUCT DEFINITION
======================================================================

PRODUCT NAME: SaathiShield AI

TAGLINE: “Your Digital Safety Companion.”

PRODUCT CONCEPT: A Hindi-first, multilingual digital-safety assistant
that helps people understand common online and financial scams,
recognize suspicious messages and payment requests, and find safe,
official next steps.

CORE PROBLEM: Many first-time or less digitally confident users
encounter UPI scams, fake KYC messages, phishing links, OTP requests,
impersonation scams and other online fraud. They may not understand
technical warnings or know which official channel to use. The product
should make reliable safety guidance easier to understand and act on.

IMPORTANT POSITIONING: Hindi is a widely supported language. Do NOT
claim that Hindi itself is an underserved language. Position the
underserved focus around the specific user domain: first-time
digital-payment users and people who need simple, accessible,
trustworthy cybersecurity guidance.

TARGET USERS: - First-time UPI and digital-payment users. - Rural and
semi-urban users. - Elderly users and people with limited digital
confidence. - People who struggle with technical banking and
cybersecurity terms. - General Indian users who need clear
scam-awareness guidance.

LANGUAGES: - Primary: Hindi in Devanagari. - Secondary: Hinglish in
natural Roman Hindi. - Additional: English. Provide an explicit language
selector. Do not assume every Indian user understands Hindi equally
well.

PRODUCT PRINCIPLES: - Safety before engagement. - Clear steps before
technical explanations. - Trustworthy sources before confident-sounding
answers. - Privacy by default. - No false guarantees. - Accessible
design for mobile and desktop. - Transparent limitations.

The product is an educational assistance tool. It is NOT a bank, police
service, government portal, legal adviser or guaranteed fraud-recovery
service. Never imply official affiliation or endorsement.
